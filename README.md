# Frontier Clinical AI

Landing page for the Frontier Clinical AI.

## About

A weekly journal club for researchers at the intersection of AI and medicine, unapologetically technical and uncompromisingly clinical. Each week pairs classic and state-of-the-art papers within one of three areas:

1. Patient World Models and Digital Twins
2. Agentic and Embodied Clinical Intelligence
3. Clinical Translation and Post-Deployment Monitoring
