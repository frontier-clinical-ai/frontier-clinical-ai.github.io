Frontier Clinical AI Journal Club

Launching Summer 2027

AI is moving fast, but it’s proven hard to translate into routine clinical workflows, and harder still to turn into improvements in patient outcomes. Each week, we pair a classic paper with a frontier one, work through the open problems, and ask what it would take to build a safe and robust path that gets today’s AI research to patients and doctors faster.
